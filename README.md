## introduction
ArsenSafe is a portable arsenic detection technology designed to identify arsenic contamination in drinking water quickly and conveniently at the point of use. The system is based on a graphene-based sensing platform and is intended to provide rapid and reliable detection without requiring sophisticated laboratory infrastructure. ArsenSafe can be particularly useful in regions where groundwater contamination by arsenic is a serious concern, as prolonged exposure to arsenic-contaminated water can pose significant risks to human health. By enabling on-site monitoring, the technology aims to support timely identification of contaminated water sources and help improve access to safer drinking water.
